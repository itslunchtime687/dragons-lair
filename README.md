# dragons-lair
Investigate the lair with your laser and clipboard
Dragon's Lair 🐉✨

A clockwork mirror-maze puzzle game starring Brandi Sparkles, Sparkientist Scientist, who is on a mission to make dragons fabulous through science.

Bounce your glitter laser off mirrors, turn gear cranks, and send your team (Mel, Aileen and Pippen) after the laser dot to light every dragon crystal. Work your way through 9 floors of attics, wings and basements to soothe the grumpy dragon at the bottom of the lair.

Play

Open index.html in any modern browser, or play it online through GitHub Pages.

Phone: D-pad to move · ✋ Action · ⚡ Laser
Keyboard: Arrows/WASD move · Space action · L laser · M map view · C clipboard · B backpack · N mute

Stuck? Open the clipboard and tap 📱 Call Nikki, or ▶ Watch Brandi solve it.
